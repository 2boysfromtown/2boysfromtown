Match Report

Rohit, Bumrah fire as MI beat RCB in a last-over Wankhede classic

3 May 2024 · By MI Fan Portal Editorial

Mumbai Indians produced a clinical team performance at a packed Wankhede Stadium on Friday night, defeating Royal Challengers Bengaluru by seven wickets in a match that seemed closer than the scoreline suggests.

Jasprit Bumrah, operating at his intimidating best, bowled a maiden over in the powerplay before removing Virat Kohli for a well-made 52 with an unplayable yorker. His final figures of 3/22 from four overs strangled the RCB innings in its final phase, restricting them to 165/8 from their 20 overs despite a late surge from Dinesh Karthik.

In reply, Rohit Sharma batted with the kind of authority that reminds the IPL of why he remains, at 37, one of the most dangerous openers in T20 cricket. His 68 off 43 — featuring four sixes and six fours — set MI on course and was the central pillar of the chase. Even after his departure with 40 runs still needed from 27 balls, Suryakumar Yadav held his nerve to guide MI home with two balls to spare.

"We stayed calm. The wicket was doing a little bit and Bumrah gave us the perfect start. Job done," said Rohit Sharma at the post-match presentation.
