Who We Are

The Most Successful Franchise in IPL History

Mumbai Indians are the flagship franchise of the Indian Premier League, owned by Reliance Industries through IndiaWin Sports. Since their IPL debut in 2008, MI have grown into the benchmark of excellence in the shortest format of the game.

With five IPL trophies — won in 2013, 2015, 2017, 2019, and 2020 — and consistent playoff appearances, Mumbai Indians have built a legacy unmatched by any other franchise. Their home ground, the iconic Wankhede Stadium, is one of cricket's most electric venues.

What sets MI apart is not just results but culture: a relentless investment in young Indian talent, a playing philosophy built on pace bowling and aggressive batting, and a fan base that stretches far beyond Mumbai to every corner of India and the globe.

Rohit Sharma has captained Mumbai Indians to four of their five IPL titles. His ability to stay calm under pressure, read conditions expertly, and get the best from world-class talent makes him one of cricket's finest leaders. As a batsman, Rohit's 6,600+ IPL runs and signature pulls and pulls are the stuff of legend at Wankhede.

Head Coach

Mark Boucher

Former South Africa wicketkeeper-batsman Mark Boucher took over as MI Head Coach ahead of IPL 2024. Known for his meticulous preparation, Boucher brings international coaching experience from his time with South Africa's national team. He has placed an emphasis on aggressive cricket and building a strong fast-bowling unit around Bumrah.

Journey

A Timeline of Glory

2008

The Beginning

Mumbai Indians enter the IPL for the first season. Sachin Tendulkar leads the team for the first few seasons, setting the foundation for the franchise's culture of excellence. They finish 5th in the inaugural edition.

2010

First Final

MI reach the IPL final for the first time but fall short against Chennai Super Kings. The near-miss galvanizes the squad and management to build more purposefully for a title charge.

2013

Champions at Last 🏆

Rohit Sharma's first full season as captain ends with Mumbai Indians lifting their maiden IPL title, defeating Chennai Super Kings in a nail-biting final at Eden Gardens. Kieron Pollard's late blitz seals the win.

2014

Runners-up

MI defend brilliantly all tournament but fall to Kolkata Knight Riders in the final. The team starts to build around the nucleus of Rohit, Bumrah (who joins in 2013), and Pollard.

2015

Back-to-Back Champions 🏆

Mumbai Indians clinch their second title with a last-ball victory over Chennai Super Kings. Lendl Simmons' explosive 68* off 45 balls in the final is one of the greatest IPL knocks. Two titles in three years.

2017

A Third Crown 🏆

With Krunal Pandya's astonishing 47* off 38 in the final against Rising Pune Supergiant, MI win by just one run in the closest IPL final in history. Rohit Sharma cements his reputation as the best T20 captain in the world.

2019

Record Fourth Title 🏆

A superb team performance throughout the season culminates in a brilliant final against Chennai Super Kings. Lasith Malinga's last-ball dismissal of Shardul Thakur seals an iconic one-run victory — making MI the first team to win four IPL titles.

2020

Five-Star Champions 🏆

Played in the UAE due to COVID-19, MI dominate the tournament and defeat Delhi Capitals in the final. Trent Boult and Bumrah are devastating throughout, and Rohit Sharma tops the run charts. A record fifth title.

2024

Rebuilding & Hunting

With Hardik Pandya returning and new coach Mark Boucher at the helm, Mumbai Indians enter IPL 2024 with fresh energy and a hunger to add a sixth title to their legendary cabinet.

Home Ground

Wankhede Stadium

Wankhede Stadium in South Mumbai is one of cricket's most storied venues. Located near the Marine Drive seafront, its distinctive bowler-friendly pitch and raucous atmosphere make it one of the hardest grounds for visiting teams.

With a capacity of roughly 33,000, Wankhede has witnessed some of the greatest moments in cricket history — none more iconic than India's 2011 World Cup final victory, lifted by MS Dhoni's six into the Mumbai night sky.

For MI, it's a fortress. Their home record at Wankhede is among the best of any IPL side at their home venue, and the crowd — the true 12th man — plays a massive role in the team's success.
