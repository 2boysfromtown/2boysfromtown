# 2BFTclaw

Personal AI agents as a service. Your own AI co-founder in 2 taps.

## What it is

A consumer product built on top of OpenClaw. Users get a personal agent with persistent memory, a real inbox, and the ability to take actions on their behalf — calendar, email, research, follow-ups.

## Who it's for

- Solo operators, indie founders, creators
- Anyone who already lives in WhatsApp/Telegram and doesn't want a new dashboard

## Distribution

- WhatsApp-first
- Telegram managed bot as the secondary surface

## Status

Private alpha. Sign up via [2bft.in](https://2bft.in).
