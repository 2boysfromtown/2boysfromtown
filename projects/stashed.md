# Stashed

Made-in-India tech bag brand from 2BFT.

## What it is

A small-batch tech accessories label — bags, sleeves, organisers — designed and manufactured in India. Drop 001 has shipped; further drops are in production.

## Why a hardware brand from an AI lab?

- We use our own bags every day; product feedback is immediate
- Revenue from Stashed funds the AI lab without VC pressure
- Operationally, Stashed is automated end-to-end by OpenClaw agents (inventory, support, marketing)
