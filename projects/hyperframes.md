# HyperFrames

HyperFrames is 2BFT's AI-generated video pipeline. It pairs model-generated frames with After Effects-style motion graphics for finished, broadcast-quality output.

## Repos

- [`2boysfromtown/hyperframes-motion`](https://github.com/2boysfromtown/hyperframes-motion) — motion graphics layer (HTML/JS)

## What it does

- Generates short-form video frames from prompts
- Composes them with motion graphics presets (lower thirds, transitions, kinetic type)
- Outputs delivery-ready clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and the 2BFT Academy library

## Why we built it

Off-the-shelf AI video tools don't compose with motion graphics the way an editor would. HyperFrames gives our team an editor-shaped workflow with model-generated content underneath.

## Status

Internal use. Motion graphics layer is public for reference and reuse.
